## What I would check, in order

1. **The seat effect (the biggest claim).** In same-fleet match-ups the same seat won both seat-swapped games in 106 of 135 pairs. Check the pairing logic in `runCell` and `rulesFor` in `tools/variants.mjs`: is "seat" really the thing held constant, or is it the starting layout (both games of a pair use the same seed, so the same layout)? I could not separate seat from layout. My guess at the cause (back-to-back turns from the alternating first player) is unproven. A second guess, found later: flagships with a bare core segment die to one 3-hit shot, so the first clean shot wins.
2. **Stage 1 is 10 games a cell.** Only 9-1 or better is labelled clear. Check that nothing in `balance-evidence.md` states a leaning cell as fact. One known noise example is written up there (the same match-up came out 1-9 and 4-6 on different seeds).
3. **The repeated run.** Stage 1A was run once with the same five seeds in every cell, then re-run with per-match-up seeds. I say I did not read the first run's win-loss tables before re-running, only the seat totals. That cannot be verified from the repository; weigh it accordingly.
4. **The missile counts.** "Caught in another blast or still flying at the end" is a remainder, not a measurement. "Shot down" counts only laser kills during a fire action.
5. **New switches are off by default.** Confirm from `beta/engine/rules.js` that `min_move`, `first_player`, `flare_rounds` and `magazine_explodes` do nothing unless the world file sets them, and that `sprint_drive` and `naval_mine` are new entries that change no existing part.
6. **Forced movement** is enforced in `turn.js` `apply` (the turn cannot end, and the last command point cannot be spent elsewhere, while a move is owed). The simulated pilots comply through a fallback in the tool (`drift`), not through the AI. The play screen has no support for it.
7. **Human-style pilots** (`scans: eager`, `target_priority: threat`, `bait_ship`) were written by me and lost or drew. A weak implementation would look the same as a weak idea. Read the code in `beta/engine/ai.js` before believing "tricks do not pay".
8. **Boards.** Starting positions were generated by a script and validated by `buildRules`, then left as hand-editable files. Computer-flown results in `ships-and-boards.md` are a smoke test only. Nobody has played these by hand. Four of ten were opened in a headless browser.
9. **Pasted-code play** was checked at the engine level only, not by clicking through the browser, and not through WhatsApp. Beta game codes are not sealed and expose both fleets.
