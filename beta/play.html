How to play Shatkon

  1. Goal: destroy the enemy flagship (marked ★). Each player has a small scout and a big flagship, built from hex segments full of parts.
  2. Your turn: you get 3 command points. Moving one ship, firing one laser, launching one missile or scanning costs 1 point each. Unspent points are banked (up to 6).
  3. Moving: tap your ship, tap a green marker to preview, then tap it again. Your fleet shares one pool of thrust; a bigger ship costs more thrust per hex.
  4. Fog of war: you only see what your ships can detect. Big ships are easier to spot, stealth parts and the purple nebula hide you, and firing or launching makes you flare up. A "?" marks where an enemy was last seen.
  5. Lasers hit instantly in a straight line (2 hits up to 4 hexes, 1 hit up to 8) and asteroids block them. Missiles fly during the Flight step after both players have moved, so lock on and plan ahead. Point defence can stop one missile a round.
  6. Detection tiers: a red fuzzy circle means something is somewhere inside it (any hex in the circle is equally likely, and the circle doesn't move while the target sits still, so looking again tells you nothing new); ◆ means you know exactly where a ship is; ▣ means a fresh scan shows every part and its hull.
  7. Scan (1 point, once per ship per turn): a ship can only scan on a turn it has moved (at least 1 hex away from where it started the turn; moving there and back doesn't count). The further forward the ship is, the stronger the scan: near your home edge it sees 2 hexes clearly and 4 faintly, in mid-field 4 / 7, and from the centre line onwards 6 / 10. Before sending it you see both rings and how much further forward the next tier is. Everything in the green ring is fully revealed, hidden mines included; the amber ring only picks up faint contacts. Scanning lights your ship up.
  8. Stealth & signature work like engines and thrust: a ship's signature starts at its size (1 per segment) and each working stealth part takes 1 off, down to a floor of segments ÷ 3 (rounded up). Firing, launching or scanning adds a flare. You're spotted when an enemy is within their sensor range + your signature, so the flagship (7 segments, 4 stealth = 3) is seen from 7 hexes instead of 11. Stealth parts are free; lose one to damage and you lose its −1.
  9. Engine wake: a big move (2+ hexes) leaves a wake (signature +1), so creeping 1 hex at a time is quieter. It lasts until that ship's next turn.
  10. Damage strips the outer layers first: shields, then armour, then weapons, then the guts. Lose the core and the ship is gone. Every hit shows an impact report. Mines sit hidden in no man's land: only a scan finds them.
  11. On maps with a beacon (gold star): start your turn with a ship touching it and no enemy ship touching it, and you score 1. First to the map's target wins; at the round limit the higher score wins. Holding it lights your ship up for everyone, so stealth won't hide you there.
  12. Your turn ends by itself when you run out of points or moves. Tap "Why these points?" to see where every point came from.

Modes (top right): play the computer, pass one device around, or play on two devices by swapping short turn codes. Desktop: mouse wheel zooms, Esc cancels, Ctrl+Z undoes a move that revealed nothing.
