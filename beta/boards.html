Ten boards to try

You are the south fleet; the computer is north. Each board is one text file in rules/boards/: edit it and reload. To play a friend by pasted codes, open the board, choose a seat in the game's own menu and send the game code; your friend does not need the board link.

1. First LightEven duel. A 4-segment flagship and a gunboat each, three rocks.Try: step a hex before you fire; keep the gunboat close. 2. The MinefieldA wall of rock with three gaps, mined. One gap is clean. New: proximity mines, couriers.Try: run a courier to the wall and scan. Only a scan shows a mine. 3. Cat and MouseExtreme stealth: fleets pass two hexes apart unseen. Two couriers each. Bigger board.Try: scout with couriers; a courier shot down lights up everything near it and the ship that shot it. 4. The StraitOne gap three hexes wide. A shielded fleet holds the far side.Try: mass first, keep one shield down two turns running, then go through together. 5. King's GuardChess: each flagship is an unarmed king. Two gunboats, a sniper and a courier fight for it.Try: decide what guards and what hunts. Find their king first. 6. Pawn StormSix one-laser skiffs and a small flagship against one dreadnought with six shields.Try: two skiffs on the same segment in one turn; the second shot gets through. 7. Beacon HillHold the beacon in the central nebula to score. Five points wins. Holding it shows where you are.Try: take the first point with the courier and cover the approaches with guns. 8. Missile AlleyOpen space. You have 18 missiles and one laser; they have point defence and shields on every ship.Try: three missiles on one ship in one round. The third lands. 9. Fog BankOutgunned two to one, but you have laid four proximity mines in the lanes and they do not know where.Try: sit behind your mines and draw them through. 10. The GambitHidden snipers that will not come to you. You have three worthless decoys.Try: give up a decoy; the sniper that shoots it shows itself. Have your guns in place first.
