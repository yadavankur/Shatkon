Rapid mode

The Grok build. You (teal, right) against the computer (pink, left). Lose your Flower and you lose.

  • You and the computer take turns, one action each. Your action plus its reply is one beat.
  • You have 15 seconds to act (the bar at the top shrinks). Then you get 5 seconds to watch the enemy's reply, but only the parts your ships can see.
  • An action is one of: step a ship 1 hex, fire one laser, launch a missile, or scan.
  • At the end of each beat, missiles fly 6 hexes, shields that weren't hit come back, and point defence reloads.
  • No passing. If the clock runs out with a laser or missile picked, it fires at the best target after a red Cancel button (1.5 seconds). If you cancel, that ship steps forward instead. If you picked a scan, or just a ship, that ship steps forward. If you picked nothing, a random Skiff or your front ship steps forward.

Tap one of your ships, then tap a glowing hex to step there, or pick Laser, Missile or Scan.
