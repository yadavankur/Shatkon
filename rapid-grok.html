    Rapid mode

    The Grok build. You (teal, right) against the computer (pink, left). Lose your Flower and you lose.

    • You and the computer take turns, one action each. Your action plus its reply is one beat.
    • You have 15 seconds to act (the shrinking timer). If the enemy does something you can see, or hits one of your ships, you get 3 seconds to watch — tap the board to skip. If they acted out of sight and nothing hit you, the watch is skipped.
    • An action is one of: move a ship (Skiffs and Darts up to 3 hexes, bigger ships up to 2, to any reachable hex in that circle), fire one laser, launch a missile, or scan.
    • At the end of each beat, missiles fly 6 hexes, shields that weren't hit come back, and point defence reloads.
    • No passing. If the clock runs out with a laser or missile picked, it fires at the best target after a red Cancel button (1.5 seconds). If you cancel, that ship moves forward instead. If you picked a scan, or just a ship, that ship moves forward. If you picked nothing, a random Skiff or your front ship moves forward.

    Tap one of your ships, then tap any glowing hex in its circle to move there, or pick Laser, Missile or Scan.
