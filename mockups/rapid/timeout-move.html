FORCED MOVE

No Passing, Ever

Rapid Mode penalises stalling by forcing a 1-hex move whenever the 15s bar hits 0.0s without a laser or missile order open.

Case A: Nothing Selected at 0.0s

The game picks one of your front singles / small ships at random (here Skiff 4 at [6,-1]) and steps it 1 hex forward toward the centre ([5,-1]).

Case B: Ship or Scan Selected at 0.0s

Scan is never auto-run. If a ship or its Scan button was open when time ran out, Scan is dropped and that selected ship steps 1 hex instead — no 1.5s red pop-up needed.
