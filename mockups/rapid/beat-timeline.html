The Beat Model: How Rounds & Timers Tick

Instead of multi-action turns, 1 Beat = 1 player action + 1 enemy action. Every round-based timer in world_v0.4.yaml hangs on the end of each beat, so missiles never jump mid-pair before you get a reply.

Step 1 · Your Action (15s)

Pick one move: step 1 hex, fire 1 laser, launch a salvo, or scan. Ends automatically — or timeout auto-fires/moves.

Step 2 · Enemy + 5s Watch

Computer acts instantly (1 action). Your controls lock for 5s so you see any enemy action inside your sight.

Step 3 · End-of-Beat Tick

Missiles fly 6 hexes; PD resets (1 stop/beat); unhit shields recharge; flares, scan-lights & afterburner burn tick down.

Missile Flight Across Beats (Speed 6, Fuel 3 Beats = 18 Hexes)

Arrow launches an Advanced missile (4 hits) on Beat 1. The opponent gets one action before the Beat 1 flight tick moves it 6 hexes — giving them time to dodge or put point defence in range.
