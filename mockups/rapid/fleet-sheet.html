Board Breakdown

  • Teal zone (109 hexes): exact sight of the enemy Flower (within 7 hexes of any petal, i.e. 8 of centre [-10,0]).
  • Red nebula pocket (5 hexes): within 7 hexes, but nebula cuts Skiff sensor 4 → 1 (-3), shrinking sight to 4 hexes and blinding it!
  • Amber ring (65 hexes): passive fuzzy contact (? circle, +3 extra range) before exact lock.
  • Asteroids: block movement and laser lines, not passive sensors.
  • Walk distance: closest front boat (Skiff 4 at [8,-1]) needs 10 single-hex moves to reach [-2,-1] and spot the Flower.
