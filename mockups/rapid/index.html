Rapid-Mode Rules Summary

  • Alternating single actions: You do one thing (move 1 hex, fire 1 laser, launch salvo, or scan) and your turn ends immediately.
  • 15s clock + 5s watch: Computer replies instantly; controls lock for 5s so you can read visible moves and flares.
  • No passing: Weapon open at 0.0s → 1.5s red Cancel then AI best safe shot (Cancel moves that ship 1 hex). Ship or scan open → that ship moves 1 hex. Nothing open → random front single moves 1 hex.

The Beat Tick (1 Beat = You + Opponent)

  • End of every beat: Missiles fly 6 hexes (3 beats fuel), downed shields recharge if unhit all beat, point defence resets (1 stop/beat), and flares/scans/afterburners tick.
  • Engines: No command points or thrust bank; any working engine lets a ship step 1 hex per move action.
  • v1 scope: Flower is flagship; fleets mirrored; no mines in v1.
